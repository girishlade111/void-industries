THE BRIEF

A FESTIVAL SITE THAT HITS LIKE THE LINEUP.

Static Field is a three-day experimental music and art festival. Their old site sold tickets but communicated nothing about the experience — it could've been a conference.

We built a site that behaves like the event: abrasive typography, scroll-driven chaos that resolves into clarity when you need schedule info, and a ticket flow that never leaves the aesthetic.

"Loud everywhere, functional where it counts — especially checkout."

GSAP-driven scenes handle the narrative sections; the schedule and ticketing are deliberately plain, fast, and keyboard-friendly. Art direction and conversion don't have to be enemies.

WHO WE ARE

EIGHT HUMANS.
ZERO FILLER.

We started VOID in a room with bad lighting and a shared hatred of template culture. Five years later we're still small on purpose — close enough to fight about kerning, big enough to ship systems that scale.

Our team spans design, creative code, and strategy. We don't hand off — the people who pitch the idea are the people who build it.

MANIFESTO / 002

FORMAT
IS A
PRISON.

Six positions we will not negotiate. Read them as rules of engagement, not suggestions.

01

Refuse the default

Every template is someone else's decision, frozen in time. We start from zero every time — even when zero is harder, slower, and more expensive. Especially then.

02

Make structure visible

The grid, the borders, the type scale — show them. Concealed systems feel corporate. Exposed systems feel honest, and honesty scales better than decoration.

03

Motion is meaning

Animation isn't garnish. How an element enters, exits, and responds tells the user what matters. We choreograph with intent or not at all.

04

Ship the weird thing

The safe option is usually the forgettable option. We prototype the risky direction first — because sometimes the risk turns out to be the strategy.

05

Small team, full stack

No relay races between strategy, design, and engineering. The same people hold the thread from first sketch to final deploy.

06

The work speaks. We don't.

No award bait. No thought-leadership theatre. The projects are the argument — everything else is noise.

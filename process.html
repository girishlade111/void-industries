PROCESS / 006

ZERO
TO
SHIPPED.

Four phases. No discovery theatre. Every phase ends with something you can look at, click, or argue with.

01

DIG

WEEK 1–2

We interrogate the brief, the market, and the thing you're afraid to say out loud. Output: a sharp positioning line, competitive pressure map, and a single design question worth solving.

02

BREAK

WEEK 3–5

Three divergent directions — one safe-ish, two deliberately unhinged. We build them far enough to feel real, then kill two in a room where everyone can see the bodies.

03

BUILD

WEEK 6–12

The chosen direction becomes a system: components, motion, voice, edge cases. Design and code advance together — weekly clickable builds, no surprise handoffs.

04

LAUNCH + LINGER

WEEK 13+

Ship it, then watch it live in the wild for 30 days. We fix what breaks, tune what sags, and hand over a system documented well enough that you stop needing us.
